🧭 Getting started

Three things to know. Takes a minute.

  1. Where your photos live

    Your originals stay exactly where they were — on the drive, laptop or phone you picked. Constellation reads them and keeps its own copy in a library folder on this computer; that copy is what the wall, the star map and the gallery show.

    The Personal Vault is a separate, locked folder for pictures that shouldn't hang on a shared wall. Pictures the safety check flags go there for an adult to review, and you can move any picture there yourself with “Move to Personal Vault”.

    Sign in with the family PIN to see the exact folders on this computer.
    Sign in to show folders

  2. The first sweep

    Right after setup, Constellation reads your newest ~2,000 photos first so the wall comes alive within minutes; older years fill in overnight. You don't need to keep this page open.

    Checking…

    See detailed progress

  3. Where to go next

    • The Wall — the picture-frame view for a TV or spare screen.
    • Constellation — the star map; tap a star to explore.
    • Gallery — find any photo by person, place or words.

    Lost? Move the mouse or touch the screen on any wall and a ☰ Menu button appears in the bottom-right corner.

    To add another folder or drive later, open the Constellation Setup app on this computer and choose it there.
