Constellation

Find, organise, and share your family photos without putting them in the cloud.

Get v0.1.0-rc1 View source Suggested donation

What it does

  • Ingests local originals without re-uploading.
  • AI tagging with a curator override so your corrections stick.
  • Built-in wall + screenshot-ready frames, plus audit (faces/geocode/variance).
  • Offline-first: runs on your machine, not a cloud.
